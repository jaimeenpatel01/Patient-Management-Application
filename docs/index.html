Clinic Management Application

Modern Practice Management for Physiotherapists

PhysioDesk is a comprehensive application designed for healthcare professionals to manage patient information, clinical records, appointments, medical documents, attendance, and payment records.

PhysioDesk Features

Tools for organizing everyday clinic management and patient records.

Patient Management

Maintain patient profiles, track medical history, and organize clinical information in one place.

Appointments

Schedule consultations, track appointment statuses, and manage daily clinic appointments.

Clinical Records & Media

Record consultations, clinical notes, treatment information, and manage patient-related documents and media.

Attendance

Record and manage patient attendance for clinic visits and treatment sessions.

Medical Documents

Upload and manage patient-related documents, reports, prescriptions, and medical images.

Payment Records

Maintain patient payment records including amounts, dates, payment status, and notes.
